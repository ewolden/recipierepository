## Apple Cake
### Ingredients for Apple Cake
Ingredient | Quantity | How
---|---|---
Margarine | 200 g | softened
Sugar | 250 g | plus extra to taste
Eggs | 4 |
Flour | 250 g |
Baking powder | 2 heaped tsp |
Vanilla sugar | 2 heaped tsp |
Apples | to taste | peeled and cut into wedges
Almonds | to taste | chopped

### Method for Apple Cake
#### Step 1
Let **200 g margarine** soften at room temperature for a few hours.

#### Step 2
Preheat the oven to **190°C**. Grease and line a cake tin.

#### Step 3
Beat **200 g softened margarine** with **250 g sugar** until pale.

#### Step 4
Mix in **4 eggs**, then stir in **250 g flour**, **2 heaped tsp baking powder**, and **2 heaped tsp vanilla sugar** until combined.

#### Step 5
Toss peeled apple wedges with **sugar to taste**, then press the **apples** into the batter.

#### Step 6
Sprinkle over **sugar to taste** and **chopped almonds to taste**.

#### Step 7
Bake for **20 minutes**, then check the colour. If browning too much, place a baking tray above the cake. Bake for another **10-15 minutes**, until done.

## Notes:
Check doneness with a cake tester or skewer; it should come out clean.
