### Method for roast beef with red wine jus, cauliflower purée and potato wedges

#### Step 1
Take **1–1.2 kg beef roast** out of the fridge 30 mins before cooking. Preheat oven to 125°C. Pat dry and season with **salt to taste** and **black pepper to taste**.

#### Step 2
Heat **1 tbsp neutral oil** in a pan. Sear **1–1.2 kg beef roast** on all sides for 6–8 mins.

#### Step 3
Lower heat. Add **2 tbsp butter**, **3 crushed garlic cloves**, and **2 sprigs rosemary or thyme**. Spoon butter over the beef for 1–2 mins.

#### Step 4
Transfer beef to an ovenproof dish, pour over pan butter, and insert a thermometer. Roast at 125°C until 55–57°C for medium-rare, 60–62°C for medium, or 68–70°C for well-done.

#### Step 5
For the jus, melt **1 tbsp butter** in the same pan. Add **2 finely sliced onions** and cook over medium-low heat for 15–20 mins until caramelized. Add **1 tsp brown sugar** if needed.

#### Step 6
Add **250 ml red wine**, scrape the pan, and reduce by half. Add **350 ml beef stock**, **1 tsp balsamic vinegar**, and **black pepper to taste**. Simmer 15–25 mins until concentrated.

#### Step 7
Strain the jus for a smooth sauce, or leave onions in for a rustic sauce. Before serving, add resting juices, remove from heat, and whisk in **1 tbsp cold butter**.

#### Step 8
Cut **900 g–1 kg potatoes** into wedges. Boil in salted water for 7–8 mins, drain, and steam-dry for 5 mins.

#### Step 9
Toss potatoes with **2 tbsp olive oil**, **1 tsp paprika**, **1 tsp garlic powder**, **½ tsp dried rosemary**, **salt to taste**, and **black pepper to taste**. Spread on a tray.

#### Step 10
When beef is done, rest it under foil for 30–40 mins. Increase oven to 230°C and roast potatoes for 25–35 mins, turning once.

#### Step 11
Steam **2 small cauliflowers** with **1 peeled garlic clove** for 12–15 mins until very tender. Let steam-dry briefly.

#### Step 12
Blend cauliflower with **40 g butter**. Gradually add **75–100 ml warm heavy cream** until smooth. Season with **1–2 tsp lemon juice**, **salt to taste**, **black pepper to taste**, and optional **1 pinch nutmeg**.

#### Step 13
Cook **250–300 g cubed swede** in a covered pan with a splash of water and **salt to taste** for 5 mins.

#### Step 14
Add **3–4 sliced carrots** and cook covered for 5–7 mins until nearly tender. Remove lid and evaporate remaining water.

#### Step 15
Add **1 tbsp butter**, **1 tbsp honey**, **1 tbsp balsamic vinegar**, and **1 tsp thyme**. Cook uncovered for 4–6 mins until glossy. Season with **salt to taste** and **black pepper to taste**.

#### Step 16
Remove string from the beef and slice thinly against the grain. Serve with cauliflower purée, glazed vegetables, potato wedges, and hot red wine jus.

## Notes:
Use a meat thermometer rather than relying on cooking time. Keep the jus slightly acidic to balance the sweetness of the cauliflower purée and glazed root vegetables. Store leftovers refrigerated for up to 3 days.
