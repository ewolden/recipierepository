## Lemon-Dill Baked Salmon with Basmati Rice and Roasted Broccolini

### Ingredients for Lemon-Dill Baked Salmon with Basmati Rice and Roasted Broccolini

Ingredient | Quantity | How
---|---|---
Broccolini | 200–250 g | trimmed, thick stems split
Olive oil | 1–2 tsp |
Salt | 1 pinch |
Olive oil | 1 tbsp |
Dijon mustard | 1 tsp |
Lemon | ½ | finely zested
Fresh dill | 1 tbsp | chopped
Salmon fillets | 400–450 g | skin-side down
Black pepper | to taste |
Sour cream or plain Greek yoghurt | 100 g |
Fresh dill | 1 tbsp | chopped
Lemon juice | 1 tsp |
Water | 1–2 tsp | optional
Butter | 1 tsp |
Lemon juice | 1 squeeze |

### Method for Lemon-Dill Baked Salmon with Basmati Rice and Roasted Broccolini

#### Step 1
Heat oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan. Rinse and cook **180 g basmati rice** according to the packet, then keep covered.

#### Step 2
Toss **200–250 g broccolini** with **1–2 tsp olive oil** and **1 pinch salt**. Spread on one side of a baking tray. For thin salmon fillets, roast broccolini for 3–5 minutes first.

#### Step 3
Mix **1 tbsp olive oil**, **1 tsp Dijon mustard**, **zest of ½ lemon**, and **1 tbsp chopped fresh dill**.

#### Step 4
Place **400–450 g salmon fillets** skin-side down on the tray. Brush with glaze and season with **black pepper to taste**.

#### Step 5
Bake for 12–16 minutes, checking thin fillets after 8 minutes, until **salmon** flakes easily and **broccolini** is tender and lightly browned.

#### Step 6
Meanwhile, stir together **100 g sour cream or plain Greek yoghurt**, **1 tbsp chopped fresh dill**, and **1 tsp lemon juice**. Thin with **1–2 tsp water** if desired.

#### Step 7
Fluff the rice with **1 tsp butter** and **1 squeeze lemon juice**. Serve with salmon, broccolini, and dill sauce.

## Notes:
Remove the salmon or broccolini from the tray early if one finishes before the other. Leftovers keep refrigerated for up to 2 days.
